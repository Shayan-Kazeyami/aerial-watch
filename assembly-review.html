About the motion

A 3.5 Hz balance governs seven releases per second. The train uses measured tooth counts and aligned starting phases. The barrel drives the train and hands; winding wheels and ratchet stay held during normal running. Turn the seated crown left to drive the winding gears and click; a right turn lets the clutch return. The calendar advances one step each day. Slow motion scales running movement. This is a visual motion study with sampled clearances; spring stress, contact forces and energy transfer are not simulated.

    The open-work front is a separate design proposal. The R07 front uses five rounded gear openings, a raised plate rim and a blank calendar surface. Original calendar teeth and measured bearing seats are retained. The original 166-mesh OM10 and solid-dial assembly remain available with Open-work front switched off.

    Materials and animation are visual studies. Structural loads, tolerances, sealing and manufacture still require validation.
