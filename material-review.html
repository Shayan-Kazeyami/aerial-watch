How this comparison is made

Scene dimensions are millimetres. The same coupon geometry, 0.06 mm bevel, camera, exposure and colour conversion are used for every material. Close-ups place each selected sample at the same point under the lights. Neutral uses white reflection cards and white lights. Product uses the watch’s photographic cards and warm/cool light palette. Surfaces use the watch’s shared finish library. The board is static and requests 2.5× resolution with up to 4× MSAA. Higher native pixel density is preserved within image-size and device limits; actual render dimensions are reported above.

These are authored appearance proposals. Coupon body thickness is 0.8 mm plus the bevel. Ruby uses an opaque coloured dielectric approximation so the main watch’s sapphire background retains the jewels; internal jewel transmission is not simulated. They are not measured alloy spectra, colour standards or production finish specifications. The two views compare appearance; they do not certify real materials.

Save image downloads the clean render. Save view downloads its JSON settings. A saved view can also be reproduced with the query parameters ?light=neutral&sample=satin; omit sample for the complete board.
