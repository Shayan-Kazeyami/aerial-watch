A NOTE ON THIS STUDY

The heart of a watch.

Technical sheet · 2021 ↗
Release notes ↗

OM10 is a mechanical movement, not a complete wristwatch. The supplied files do not provide a finished external case, crystal, dial, hands or strap. The separate holder download is a workshop fixture. The motion study uses the provisional 2021 sheet: a 3.5 Hz balance and documented average wheel speeds. Balance amplitude, spring behaviour, smooth escape-wheel rotation, exploded spacing and metallic finishes are illustrative. This is not a service guide or a validated mechanical simulation.
