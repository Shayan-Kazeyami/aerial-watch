02 / THE CHOSEN DIRECTION

Open by design.
Real at its heart.

Aerial pairs a narrow polished bezel and sculpted steel lugs with blue hands and charcoal leather. The open dial is adapted to the real OM10: the seconds indication sits on its actual offset axis, rather than copying the imagined mechanism in the concept image.

The case, crystal, bezel, dial framework, hands, retaining ring, exhibition back, crown, straps and fastening hardware are separate assemblies. Every imported movement mesh keeps its own identity.

SOURCE GEOMETRY / PRESENTATION CHOICES

Look through the layers.

The original OM10 has a solid main plate and calendar covers. “Cutaway on” hides selected plate and bridge meshes to reveal genuine components beneath. Switch it off to inspect the intact assembly; no source mesh is cut or replaced. Selecting a hidden part temporarily reveals it.

The movement geometry comes from your STEP downloads. The new Aerial exterior, exploded spacing, leather surface and metallic finishes are original presentation work. Motion uses rates from the provisional 2021 sheet, with illustrative balance amplitude and continuous escape-wheel rotation.

VISUAL PROTOTYPE / CONCEPT 04

From image to geometry.

The concept envelope provides radial clearance around the original main plate, aligns the crown with the supplied winding stem, and leaves space for front hands and rear geometry. Seals, threads, hand attachment, support loading, wrist comfort, water resistance and manufacturing tolerances remain engineering work.
